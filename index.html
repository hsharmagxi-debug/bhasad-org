Origin Story

Born in Najafgarh.
Built for the world.

Started with a dream and a dial-up connection in South West Delhi. Himanshu Sharma — known across the internet as the architect behind BHASAD — has spent over a decade converting curiosity into infrastructure.

From cable networks in Najafgarh to supporting NHL broadcast operations via Presidio on the other side of the planet, every role has been a rung on a single ladder: build things that matter at scale.

Today, BHASAD is the holding org for 12 active ventures across SaaS, AI, FinTech, Security, Media and Automation — each one a bet on the future, each one built by one person, with obsession, not permission.
